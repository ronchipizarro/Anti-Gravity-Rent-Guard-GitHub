# RENT GUARD — FDCPA-COMPLIANT COLLECTION LETTER TEMPLATES

**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-19

These templates comply with the Federal Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA, 15 U.S.C. 1692 et seq.) and the Florida Consumer Collection Practices Act (FCCPA, FL Stat. 559.55-559.785).

**Usage notes:**
- Send Letter 1 first, by certified mail AND email
- Wait 30 days before taking further collection action (FDCPA validation period)
- If tenant disputes within 30 days, send Letter 2 (Verification Response) before continuing
- If no dispute within 30 days, proceed with collection (agency referral, credit reporting, litigation)
- ALL collection communications must include the Mini-Miranda disclosure
- Do NOT contact tenants before 8:00 AM or after 9:00 PM local time
- Do NOT contact tenants at their workplace if you know the employer prohibits it
- Do NOT use threatening, abusive, or harassing language

---

## QUICK REFERENCE: FDCPA COMPLIANCE RULES

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Mini-Miranda** | EVERY written and oral communication must include: "This is an attempt to collect a debt. Any information obtained will be used for that purpose." |
| **Validation notice** | Initial communication must state: amount owed, name of creditor, 30-day dispute right, verification right, original creditor right |
| **Timing** | No contact before 8 AM or after 9 PM local time |
| **Workplace** | Do not contact at workplace if you know employer prohibits it |
| **Third parties** | Cannot disclose the debt to third parties (except spouse, parent of minor, attorney, or with consent). Can contact third parties ONCE to obtain location information only. |
| **Cease contact** | If tenant sends written request to cease contact, you must stop (except to notify of specific actions like lawsuit) |
| **Dispute response** | If tenant disputes within 30 days, CEASE all collection until verification is mailed |
| **Harassment** | No threats of violence, obscene language, repeated calls intended to harass |
| **False representations** | Cannot misrepresent the amount, legal status, or consequences of the debt |
| **Unfair practices** | Cannot collect unauthorized fees, deposit post-dated checks early, or threaten actions you don't intend to take |

### Florida-Specific (FCCPA) Additional Rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Simulation of legal process** | Cannot send documents that simulate court papers |
| **Communication restrictions** | Cannot communicate with debtor's employer about the debt (except for garnishment) |
| **Willful noncompliance** | FL allows statutory damages of up to $1,000 per violation (FL Stat. 559.77) |
| **Attorney's fees** | Debtor can recover attorney's fees if they prevail on an FCCPA claim |
