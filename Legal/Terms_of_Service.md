## 2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES

Rent Guard provides **administrative rent protection services** to residential landlords in Florida. Our services include:

- **Tenant screening and risk assessment** using proprietary underwriting criteria
- **Rent default protection** — upon a verified tenant default, Rent Guard may elect to pay the landlord protected amounts from its own operating funds
- **Eviction coordination** — management and facilitation of eviction proceedings through licensed Florida attorneys
- **Debt recovery** — collection of subrogated amounts from defaulting tenants
