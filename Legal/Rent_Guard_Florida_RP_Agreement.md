### ARTICLE I -- DEFINITIONS

**1.1 "Service Activation Event"** means any failure by Tenant to pay Rent or any other monetary obligation due under the Lease within three (3) calendar days of the applicable due date.

**1.2 "Protected Funds"** means the amounts Service Provider agrees to pay Landlord upon a verified Service Activation Event, limited to: (a) monthly base rent as stated in the Lease; and (b) reasonable eviction-related costs as determined by Service Provider in its sole discretion.

**1.3 "Service Fee"** means the non-refundable fee paid to Service Provider for the Services, as set forth in Article VIII.

**1.4 "Service Funds"** means funds disbursed by Service Provider exclusively from its own general operating funds, without the creation of reserves, mutualization of risks, or collection of premiums from any pool of participants.

**1.5 "Claim"** means a formal notification by Landlord to Service Provider, submitted through the Platform or in writing, reporting a Service Activation Event and requesting payment of Protected Funds.

**1.6 "Platform"** means the Service Provider's online portal located at [app.rentguard.com] or such other URL as Service Provider may designate, through which Claims, notices, and communications under this Agreement are processed.

**1.7 "Eviction Proceedings"** means any legal action commenced under Florida Statutes Chapter 83, Part II (Residential Tenancies), including but not limited to the filing of complaints for eviction, motions for default judgment, and applications for writs of possession.

**1.8 "Summary Procedure"** means the expedited judicial process available under Florida Statutes Section 51.011, as applicable to actions for possession of residential property.

**1.9 "Holdover Period"** means the period following the expiration or termination of the Lease during which Tenant remains in possession of the Property without Landlord's consent. Protection under this Agreement during any Holdover Period shall not exceed one hundred eighty (180) calendar days.

**1.10 "Subrogation"** means the automatic transfer to Service Provider of all of Landlord's rights, claims, and causes of action against Tenant (and any guarantor or co-signer) arising from or related to the Service Activation Event, upon Service Provider's payment of Protected Funds to Landlord.

**1.11 "Accumulated Default"** means a condition in which Tenant's unpaid monetary obligations under the Lease exceed one (1) full month's rent at the time Landlord submits a Claim. An Accumulated Default voids Service Provider's obligation to pay Protected Funds for that Claim.

**1.12 "Force Majeure"** means any event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party, including but not limited to: natural disasters, hurricanes, floods, pandemics, epidemics, government-imposed restrictions, moratoriums on evictions, acts of war or terrorism, or civil unrest.

**1.13 "Lease Commencement Date"** means the date on which Tenant's obligation to pay rent under the Lease first begins, as stated in the Recitals.

### ARTICLE III -- SCOPE OF PROTECTION

**3.1 Protected Obligations.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Service Provider agrees to pay Landlord Protected Funds upon the occurrence of a verified Service Activation Event. Protected Funds are limited to:

(a) Monthly base rent as stated in the Lease (not to exceed the amount stated in the Recitals);
(b) Reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs incurred in Eviction Proceedings initiated by Service Provider under this Agreement; and
(c) Holdover rent for a period not to exceed one hundred eighty (180) calendar days following lease expiration or termination.

**3.2 Maximum Aggregate Liability.** Service Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed **[twelve (12) months' base rent / $___________]** (the "Aggregate Cap") during the Term of this Agreement, inclusive of all Protected Funds, eviction costs, and Holdover Period payments.

**3.3 Exclusions.** Service Provider shall have NO obligation to pay, reimburse, or cover any of the following, regardless of whether such amounts are owed under the Lease:

(a) Security deposits or any portion thereof;
(b) Utility charges of any kind, including water, electric, gas, sewer, trash, internet, cable, telephone, landscaping, or pool maintenance;
(c) Property damages, whether structural, cosmetic, or otherwise, including but not limited to: normal wear and tear, negligence, vandalism, intentional destruction, or damage caused by acts of God;
(d) Homeowners association (HOA), condominium association, or community association fees or assessments;
(e) Fines, penalties, or punitive damages of any nature;
(f) Early lease termination fees or lease buyout amounts;
(g) Consequential, indirect, incidental, special, or loss-of-profit damages;
(h) Amounts arising from lease amendments, modifications, or addenda not disclosed to and approved in writing by Service Provider prior to the Service Activation Event;
(i) Pre-existing defaults -- any monetary obligation that was past due at the time this Agreement became effective;
(j) Losses arising from Force Majeure events, including but not limited to government-imposed eviction moratoriums;
(k) Insurance deductibles, insurance premiums, or any insurance-related costs;
(l) Real property taxes, special assessments, or government liens;
(m) Any amounts in excess of the Aggregate Cap; and
(n) Any obligation for which Landlord has failed to comply with the Claim procedures set forth in Article IV.

**3.4 Accumulated Defaults.** If, at the time Landlord submits a Claim, Tenant's unpaid monetary obligations under the Lease exceed one (1) full month's base rent (an Accumulated Default), Service Provider's obligation to pay Protected Funds for that Claim is void. Landlord must submit Claims within thirty (30) calendar days of the first missed payment giving rise to the Service Activation Event. Late reporting beyond thirty (30) days releases Service Provider from its payment obligation for that event.

### ARTICLE IV -- CLAIM PROCESS

**4.1 Claim Submission.** Upon the occurrence of a Service Activation Event, Landlord shall submit a Claim to Service Provider through the Platform or, if the Platform is unavailable, by email to [claims@rentguard.com] with the subject line "CLAIM -- [Property Address]." The Claim must be submitted within ten (10) calendar days of the missed payment date.

**4.2 Required Documentation.** Each Claim must include:

(a) A copy of the executed Lease (if not previously provided);
(b) Evidence of the missed payment (bank statement, payment platform screenshot, or similar documentation);
(c) Proof that the 3-Day Notice to Pay or Quit was properly served on Tenant in accordance with Florida Statutes Section 83.56 (or confirmation that Service Provider served such notice under Article V); and
(d) A signed declaration by Landlord that no partial payment has been accepted from Tenant after the Service Activation Event, and that Landlord has not entered into any side agreement with Tenant regarding the missed payment.

**4.3 Verification and Payout Timeline.** Upon receipt of a complete Claim:

(a) Service Provider shall verify the Claim within five (5) business days;
(b) If the Claim is approved, Service Provider shall disburse Protected Funds to Landlord within seven (7) business days of verification, via ACH transfer, electronic funds transfer, or such other method as Service Provider may select in its sole discretion; and
(c) Service Provider may, in its sole discretion, elect to disburse Protected Funds on an expedited basis prior to completion of full verification, reserving the right to recover such funds from Landlord if the Claim is subsequently determined to be invalid.

**4.4 Claim Denial.** Service Provider may deny a Claim if:

(a) The Claim does not meet the requirements of this Article IV;
(b) An Accumulated Default exists (Section 3.4);
(c) The loss falls within the Exclusions (Section 3.3);
(d) Landlord has breached any obligation under this Agreement;
(e) Tenant's application contained material misrepresentations that were not discoverable by Service Provider at the time of underwriting; or
(f) A Force Majeure event has occurred that prevents or delays Eviction Proceedings.
