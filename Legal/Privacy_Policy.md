## 5. FAIR CREDIT REPORTING ACT (FCRA) COMPLIANCE

### 5.1 Consumer Reports
We may obtain consumer reports (credit reports) about tenant applicants from one or more consumer reporting agencies. We do so only with the applicant's written authorization and only for a permissible purpose under the FCRA.

### 5.2 Adverse Action Notices
If we take adverse action against a tenant applicant based in whole or in part on information contained in a consumer report (e.g., denial of application, assignment to a higher-risk tier, requirement for a cosigner), we will:

(a) Provide the applicant with an **Adverse Action Notice** identifying:
- The consumer reporting agency that provided the report
- The applicant's right to obtain a free copy of the report within 60 days
- The applicant's right to dispute the accuracy of the report with the reporting agency

(b) The adverse action notice will be delivered by email to the address provided in the application.

### 5.3 Furnishing Information to Credit Bureaus
If we report tenant default information to credit bureaus (as a data furnisher), we will:

(a) Ensure the accuracy of all reported information
(b) Promptly investigate any dispute raised by the consumer or forwarded by the credit bureau
(c) Correct or delete any information found to be inaccurate
(d) Comply with all FCRA furnisher obligations under 15 U.S.C. 1681s-2

### 5.4 Your FCRA Rights
Under the FCRA, you have the right to:
- Know what is in your consumer report
- Dispute incomplete or inaccurate information
- Have consumer reporting agencies correct or delete inaccurate information
- Request that your credit report not be provided to employers (without your consent)
- Seek damages from violators

For more information, visit the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau at **consumerfinance.gov**.

## 7. DATA SECURITY

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information, including:

- **Encryption in transit**: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers uses TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS)
- **Encryption at rest**: Sensitive data (including SSNs) is encrypted in our database
- **Access controls**: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis
- **Secure hosting**: Our database and file storage are hosted on Supabase, which maintains SOC 2 Type II compliance
- **Password protection**: The underwriting dashboard is protected by authentication credentials

**However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.** We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials.

### 7.1 SSN Protection
Social Security Numbers are treated with heightened security:
- SSNs are encrypted at rest in our database
- SSN access is restricted to the underwriting process only
- SSNs are never displayed in full in the Platform interface
- SSNs are not included in emails or external communications
- SSNs are retained only as long as necessary for underwriting and compliance purposes
