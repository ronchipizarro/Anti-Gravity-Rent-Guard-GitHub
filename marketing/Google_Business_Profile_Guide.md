## 1. Address Options — Resolve This First

This is the biggest blocker before you can create your profile. Google requires a verifiable address, but you have options.

### Can you use Google Business Profile without a physical address?

Yes. Google offers a **Service Area Business (SAB)** profile type specifically for businesses that serve customers at their location rather than a storefront. For a SAB, you:

- Enter an address for verification purposes only (postcard mailed there, or alternative verification)
- Hide that address from public view after verification
- Publish a service area (Miami-Dade County) instead of a street address

This is the correct profile type for RentGuard at this stage.

---

### Option A: Service Area Business — No Address Listed (Free)

**How it works:**
1. During setup, enter any address where you can receive mail (home, friend's address, virtual office)
2. Google verifies via postcard to that address
3. After verification, go to your profile settings and toggle off "Show business address"
4. Enter Miami-Dade County as your service area

**Pros:** Zero cost, fastest to launch
**Cons:** Some Google ranking signals favor businesses with a listed address; you cannot appear in "near me" searches tied to a specific neighborhood

**Best for:** MVP launch before you have a real office

---

### Option B: Virtual Office in Miami-Dade (~$50–150/month)

A virtual office gives you a real Miami street address for your GBP, which can improve local ranking signals and lend credibility to a B2B financial services product.

**What to look for:**
- A unique suite number (not a shared mailbox number like PMB #123 — Google may reject these)
- A building with receptionist services or mail handling
- Proof that the address is a recognized business address (not a UPS Store or FedEx Office — Google bans those)
- Option to receive your Google verification postcard there

**Specific providers in Miami-Dade:**

| Provider | Approximate Cost/Month | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Regus** | $89–$150 | Multiple Miami locations (Brickell, Coral Gables, Doral, Aventura); well-known, accepted by Google |
| **WeWork** | $99–$149 | Brickell City Centre and other Miami locations; premium brand credibility |
| **Alliance Virtual Offices** | $49–$79 | Budget option; Miami addresses available; review if address is unique enough for Google |
| **iPostal1** | $9.99–$49.99 | Digital mailbox service — **caution:** some iPostal1 addresses are retail mailbox locations; verify the specific address is Google-compliant before committing |
| **Opus Virtual Offices** | $49–$65 | Miami-Dade addresses; dedicated suite numbers |

**Key question to ask any provider:** "Is this a dedicated suite number at a real office building, and have other clients successfully used this address for Google Business Profile verification?"

---

### Option C: Home Address (Not Recommended for MVP)

Using a personal home address is technically allowed for a SAB if you hide it post-verification. However:

- Your full home address is submitted to Google during setup and stored in their system
- If you accidentally make the address public (easy to do during profile edits), it appears in Google Search and Maps
- Landlord clients searching your business will see a residential address, which undermines credibility for a financial services product
- Once submitted to Google, removing or changing an address requires re-verification

**Recommendation: Do not use your home address.**

---

### Recommendation for MVP

**Use a Regus or Opus Virtual Office address in Miami ($49–$89/month).** The cost is negligible relative to the credibility boost for a B2B financial services product targeting landlords. Regus addresses in Brickell or Coral Gables are recognized by Google and carry a professional business image. Set up the virtual office first, then begin GBP setup.

If you need to launch immediately before the virtual office is ready, use the Service Area Business approach with a temporary address and plan to update to the virtual office address after verification.

## 2. Step-by-Step Profile Setup

### Prerequisites

- Google Workspace account active (rentguard.us.com added as secondary domain)
- Verified address ready (virtual office or home)
- Phone number for the business (can be a Google Voice number if you don't have a dedicated line yet)
- Website live at rentguard.us.com

---

### Step 1: Start Your Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and sign in with your Google Workspace account (the one tied to rentguard.us.com, not a personal Gmail)
2. Click **Manage now** or **Add your business**
3. Enter the business name: **RentGuard**

---

### Step 2: Select Business Category

Google's categories are fixed — you cannot create custom ones. The category signals what searches your profile appears in.

**Primary category (select exactly this):**
`Financial Services`

If that is not available or you want a more specific option, check these in order of preference:
1. `Financial Consultant` — positions you as an advisory service
2. `Real Estate Agency` — broad, but lands you in real estate searches
3. `Property Management Company` — if you want to reach landlords searching for management help

**Do not select:**
- Insurance Agency (you are not insurance)
- Legal Services (you are not a law firm)

**Secondary categories (add after initial setup, under Edit Profile > Categories):**
- `Real Estate Consultant`
- `Tenant Screening Service` (if available)

---

### Step 3: Address and Service Area

1. When asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?", select **No** (unless you have a virtual office with walk-in availability — most do not)
2. You will be prompted for a service area instead
3. Enter: **Miami-Dade County, Florida**
4. You can also add specific cities: Miami, Hialeah, Coral Gables, Doral, Homestead, Miami Beach, Miami Gardens, North Miami

If you used a virtual office address, you may be asked to enter it here. Enter it, complete verification, then decide whether to show or hide it.

---

### Step 4: Contact Information

- **Website:** `https://rentguard.us.com`
- **Phone:** Use a dedicated business number. Options:
  - Google Voice number (free, forwards to your cell) — adequate for MVP
  - Grasshopper or OpenPhone ($15–$25/month) — gives you a local Miami 305/786 area code, looks more professional in local search results
  - A local Miami area code (305 or 786) will slightly outperform an out-of-area number for Miami-Dade local searches

Serving Miami-Dade County, FL.

## 3. Verification Process

### Available Verification Methods

Google determines which methods are available based on your business type and location. For a service-area business with no listed storefront, expect:

| Method | Availability for SAB | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Postcard** | Almost always offered | 5–14 business days |
| **Phone/SMS** | Sometimes offered | Immediate |
| **Video call** | Increasingly common for new businesses | Scheduled within a few days |
| **Email** | Rare for new profiles | Immediate if offered |
| **Instant** | Only for businesses Google can already verify (established brands) | Not applicable for new profile |

---

### What to Expect

1. After completing setup, Google will show you available verification options
2. **Most likely for a new SAB:** You will be offered a postcard sent to your registered address, or a video verification
3. **Postcard:** Google mails a card with a 5-digit PIN to your address. Enter the PIN in your GBP dashboard. Do not change your business name, address, or category before the postcard arrives — this resets the verification clock
4. **Video verification:** A Google representative video calls you and asks you to show your workspace and confirm your business details. Have your website, a business card or logo, and your address ready. This can be done at a virtual office or your home office — just be prepared to show the space
5. Your profile is limited (low visibility, cannot respond to reviews) until verification is complete

---

### Timeline

- Virtual office setup: 1–3 business days
- GBP profile creation: 30 minutes
- Postcard arrival: 5–14 business days (Miami delivery typically 7–10 days)
- Total from start to live profile: 2–3 weeks if using postcard; faster if phone or video verification is offered

**Tip:** Check your GBP dashboard daily after submission. If video or phone verification becomes available while waiting for the postcard, take it immediately to save time.

## 5. Local SEO Quick Wins

### GBP Ranking Signals That Matter Most

Google ranks local business profiles on three factors: **Relevance**, **Distance**, and **Prominence**.

1. **Category accuracy** — Your primary category (`Financial Services`) must match what people search. Do not add irrelevant categories to cast a wider net; it dilutes relevance.

2. **NAP consistency** — Your business Name, Address (or service area), and Phone number must be identical across your GBP, website, and every directory you list on. Even small differences (St. vs Street, Suite vs Ste) can hurt ranking.

3. **Review velocity and rating** — More reviews, accumulated consistently over time, rank higher than a burst of reviews. A 4.5-star average with 20 reviews outranks a 5-star average with 3 reviews.

4. **Post frequency** — Active profiles (weekly posts) rank higher than stale ones.

5. **Profile completeness** — Fill in every field: hours, description, photos, products, attributes, services. Google rewards complete profiles.

6. **Keyword presence** — Include "Miami-Dade landlord," "rent protection," and "Florida rental" naturally in your description, posts, and responses to reviews.

7. **Engagement signals** — Click-through rates from search results, calls from GBP, and requests for directions all signal relevance.

---

### How to Get Your First 5 Reviews (Ethical Approaches)

Google's terms prohibit incentivizing reviews (do not offer discounts or gifts in exchange). These approaches are compliant:

1. **Direct ask — early customers:** For any landlord who signs up, send a personal email 2 weeks after they join asking for an honest review. Include your direct GBP review link (get this from your GBP dashboard under "Get more reviews"). Keep the ask simple: "If you have 2 minutes, an honest review on Google would mean a lot to us as a new business."

2. **Personal network — colleagues and advisors:** People who have worked with you professionally can leave a review based on their knowledge of your professionalism and expertise, even if they have not used the product. Ask 3–5 colleagues, investors, or advisors individually — not as a mass blast.

3. **Real estate agent partners:** If you are actively outreaching to agents (per your lead-gen campaigns), any agent who has had a conversation with you and found it valuable can leave a review. Ask in a follow-up email after a positive call.

4. **Response to every review:** Respond to all 5 of your first reviews within 24 hours. This signals to Google that the profile is actively managed and increases the likelihood the reviews are retained (Google sometimes filters reviews from inactive profiles).

5. **QR code at any in-person interaction:** If you meet a landlord or agent at a networking event, have a QR code (generated from your GBP "Get more reviews" link) on a business card or phone screen. Ask in the moment.
