## Usage Notes

- **LinkedIn personal posts** should come from the founder's profile, not the company page, for the first 30–60 days. Personal posts get significantly more organic reach. Tag the company page in each.
- **Instagram** — prioritize Reels over static images for reach at launch. A 30–45 second Reel walking through the 3-step process (voiceover + text overlays) will outperform a static graphic on launch day.
- **WhatsApp Business** — set the profile photo to the RentGuard logo. Use a local Miami phone number if possible — it builds trust with landlords who may be cautious about services they haven't heard of before.
- **Bilingual notes** — the Miami audience is heavily bilingual. Light Spanish references (as used in Instagram Post 5) are appropriate and build connection. Full Spanish versions of key posts can be added in a Phase 2 content calendar if engagement data supports it.
- **What NOT to say** — avoid the word "insurance" in any context. RentGuard is not licensed as an insurance product. Use: "rental protection," "contractual protection," "coverage," or "protection service."
