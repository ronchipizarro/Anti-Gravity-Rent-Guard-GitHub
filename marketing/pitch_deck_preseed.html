RentGuard:

RentGuard is a B2B2C platform providing rent default guarantees to residential property owners. We underwrite tenants before lease signing, and if they default, we pay — rent + lawyers + courts — until the property is recovered.

Business Model
How We Make Money

Simple, Scalable
Premium Model.

Landlords or tenants pay an annual premium of 4%–10% of annual gross rent for 12 months of full coverage. Pricing adjusts based on tenant creditworthiness and market risk parameters.

On a $2,000/mo rental at 5% → $1,200/year per policy. At 1,000 active policies → $1.2M ARR.

  • 🏠

    B2B2C via Brokers

    Real estate agents earn a referral fee, making them active distribution partners.

  • 🏢

    B2B Direct

    Multi-family operators and REITs sign portfolio agreements with volume pricing.

  • 👤

    B2C Direct

    Individual landlords apply online. Lowest CAC via SEO and word-of-mouth.
