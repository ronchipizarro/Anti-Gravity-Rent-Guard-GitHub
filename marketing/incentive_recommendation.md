# Strategic Incentive Recommendation: Broker Activation

To turn a "passive" broker into an "active" advocate, the incentive must be **immediate, tangible, and celebrated.**

## 1. The "Launch Activation" Tier (First 3 Deals)
To ensure immediate traction, we implement a front-loaded incentive for new partners:
- **First 3 Deals:** **20% Referral Fee** (Calculated on the annual premium).
- *Logic:* This massive initial payout (4-5x the industry average) ensures the broker prioritizes RentGuard for their next three lease signings.

## 2. The Monthly Performance Tiers (Ongoing)
After the first 3 deals, the broker moves to a performance-based base rate:
- **Base Rate:** **8% Referral Fee**.
- **Bronze (2 Deals in a month):** Fee increases to **10%** for that month.
- **Silver (3+ Deals in a month):** Fee increases to **12%** for that month.

## 3. The "Closed-Loop" Reward (Instant Gratification)
Brokers hate waiting for 30-day payout cycles.
- **Standard:** Commissions are paid via **Stripe** or **Wise** within **48 hours** of lease activation.
- *Marketing Angle:* "Close Friday, Get Paid Monday." This is a major differentiator against traditional insurance-style payouts.

## 4. Co-Marketing Grants
For high-performing brokers or agencies:
- Offer to pay for their professional photography or a "Social Media Spotlight" for their agency if they bring 3+ deals in a month.
- This aligns RentGuard with their brand growth, not just their wallet.

---

## Tracking & Execution
All incentives should be visible in the **Broker Portal** to allow them to "track their progress" toward the next tier.
