## Why RentGuard?
Traditional security deposits and "tenant screening" aren't enough when a $20,000+ eviction event hits. RentGuard turns your lease into a **guaranteed income stream**.

### 1. 100% Guaranteed Rent
If your tenant stops paying, we start paying. You receive your monthly rent throughout the entire default and eviction process. No gaps in cash flow.

### 2. Full Legal & Eviction Coverage
We handle the headaches. From the first legal notice to the final property recovery, RentGuard manages the process and covers **100% of attorney fees and court costs**.

### 3. AI-Powered Tenant Vetting
We use proprietary AI underwriting to vet tenants in under 48 hours. We look deeper than a credit score to ensure only the most reliable tenants enter your property.

### 4. Zero Cost to the Landlord
In most cases, the small annual premium (starting at 4%) is factored into the lease or covered by the tenant's move-in costs. You get the protection; the asset pays for itself.
