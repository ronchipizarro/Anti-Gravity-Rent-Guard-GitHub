# 📞 Pillar 6: Customer Support Strategy

## 📍 Objective
Provide "Founder-Level" support to Miami brokers and owners to build trust during the early launch phase, with a path to scalable automation.

---

## 🛠 Recommended Stack: "The Lean Startup Trio"
1. **WhatsApp Business API (Primary)**:
   - **Why**: 95% of Miami brokers use WhatsApp as their primary communication tool.
   - **Setup**: Centralized "Grenty Support" number.
   - **Automation**: Quick replies for: "How to Apply", "Pricing Chart", "Claim Status".
2. **Dedicated Email (Secondary)**: 
   - `support@rentguard.com` (for document transfers and formal notices).
3. **Internal Log**: 
   - `Strategy and Planning/support_faq_log.md` to track every question asked and create a Knowledge Base.

---

## 👤 Ownership & Response Times
- **Santiago (Sales)**: Handles "Pre-Sales" queries (Pillar 4).
- **Manager (Founder)**: Handles "Technical/Underwriting" disputes (Pillar 6).
- **Target Response**: < 15 minutes during Miami business hours (9 AM - 6 PM EST).

---

## 📋 Initial FAQ (To be built into WhatsApp)
- *Q: How long does approval take?* -> A: 24 hours.
- *Q: What if the tenant leaves early?* -> A: The guarantee covers the full 12-month lease term or until a new tenant is found.
- *Q: Is this a loan for the tenant?* -> A: No, it's a fee-based guarantee service. No debt is reported unless they default.
