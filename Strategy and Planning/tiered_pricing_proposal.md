# 💰 Pillar 1: Tiered Pricing & Underwriting Proposal

## 📍 Objective
Align the cost of rent protection with the risk profile of the tenant, ensuring Grenty stays profitable while remaining attractive to landlords.

---

## 🟢 GREEN TIER (Low Risk)
*AI Score: 80 - 100*
*Profile: Strong credit, stable income (3x rent), clean rental history.*

- **Pricing**: **4% of Annual Rent**.
- **Payment Options**:
  - One-time upfront (preferred).
  - Monthly (with 10% premium: ~0.37% rent/month).
- **Approval Strategy**: Auto-approve. Instant contract generation.

---

## 🟡 YELLOW TIER (Medium Risk)
*AI Score: 50 - 79*
*Profile: Moderate credit, income 2.5x-3x rent, minor hiccups.*

- **Pricing**: **6% - 8% of Annual Rent**.
- **Requirement**: May require a **Cosigner** (RentGuard Cosigner module).
- **Payment Options**: Upfront only.
- **Approval Strategy**: Manual underwriter review required.

---

## 🔴 RED TIER (High Risk / Rejected)
*AI Score: < 50*
*Profile: Eviction history, income < 2x rent, major credit issues.*

- **Pricing**: **N/A (Standard Reject)**.
- **Exception**: If the landlord insists, we can offer a **"Security Deposit Management"** service (no guarantee, just handling the deposit for a $99 flat fee).
- **Approval Strategy**: Auto-reject for Rent Protection.

---

## 📈 Strategic Economics
- **Broker Kickback**: 10% of the Grenty fee (e.g., on a $3,000 rent, Grenty collects $1,440. Broker gets $144).
- **Market Positioning**: We are the "Zero Deposit" alternative. Total move-in cost for a Green tenant = $0 + First Month Rent + Grenty Fee.
