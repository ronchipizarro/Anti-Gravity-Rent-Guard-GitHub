# ⚙️ Pillar 6: Tenant Default Workflow (Operational Manual)

## 📍 Objective
Minimize financial loss for the owner and preserve Grenty's guarantee pool by responding rapidly and structured to missed rent payments.

---

## 📅 Detection (Day 1-3)
1. **Missed Payment Alert**: Stripe/Bank notification triggers a "Payment Failed" status in the system.
2. **Grace Period Verification**: Ensure the delay isn't a simple banking error (NSF, expired card).
3. **Owner Notification**: Automated notification to the landlord: *"We have detected a payment delay. Grenty is initiating the recovery protocol."*

---

## 🏃 Internal Recovery (Day 4-10)
*Owner: Grenty Support / Sales Advisor*

1. **Direct Outreach (The "Soft" Touch)**: 
   - SMS/WhatsApp to the tenant: *"Hey [Name], it looks like your rent payment bounced. Can we help resolve this today?"*
   - Phone call if no response within 24 hours.
2. **The "Cure" Offer**: If the tenant is facing temporary hardship, offer a 5-day payment plan (one-time exception).
3. **Formal Notice**: Day 7 - Send a "Notice to Pay or Quit" (specific to Florida laws).

---

## 🛡️ Guarantee Invocation (Day 11-30)
*Owner: Finance / Underwriting*

1. **Owner Payout**: If payment is not recovered by Day 15, Grenty pays the owner the full amount from the guarantee pool.
2. **Tenant Status**: Tenant is moved to **"DEFAULTER"** status. Access to future Grenty services is revoked.
3. **Legal Handover**: On Day 16, the file is handed to local Miami legal partners to initiate the standard "3-Day Notice" or eviction process if necessary.

---

## 📈 Post-Default Audit
1. **Underwriting Review**: Why did the AI score this tenant as Green/Yellow?
2. **Risk Model Adjustment**: Update parameters if the default was caused by a detectable pattern (e.g., specific job type, specific area).
